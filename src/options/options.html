Where data goes

  • mail.tm: only when you create or read disposable inboxes.
  • Browserless / your endpoint: the pages you open in the Disposable Browser.
  • Your AI provider: the messages you send, plus page, e-mail or file context when you ask for an analysis.
  • Safe Browsing / VirusTotal / abuse.ch: only if enabled. Safe Browsing receives hash prefixes, not URLs.
